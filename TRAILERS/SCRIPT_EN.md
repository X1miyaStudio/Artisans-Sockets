# Artisan's Sockets — detailed trailer script

Target: 48 seconds, 16:9, 60 FPS. Tone: precise workshop craft with fast proof of five play styles.

## Capture preparation

- Use a clean English-release world with one anvil on a workshop floor.
- Prepare supported tools, weapons, and armor plus quartz, amethyst shard, diamond, emerald, and lapis lazuli.
- Keep one unsocketed copy of every demonstrated item for retakes.
- Build five small proof stations: smeltable ore, movement lane, combat dummy/mob, reach setup, and underwater mining area.
- Record item lore and attributes at a readable UI scale.

## Accuracy rules

- Show only supported vanilla items.
- Never show two gemstone paths on one item.
- Do not claim compatibility with custom equipment from other datapacks.
- Attribute tooltips must come from the release build; do not add fake numerical overlays.
- When demonstrating reach or movement, use the same camera and terrain for comparison.

## Retake checklist

- Gem visibly disappears during socketing.
- Socketed item is visibly the same base equipment item.
- Tooltip text is sharp enough to pause and read.
- Each gemstone shot uses the correct effect.

## Deliverables

- Full English trailer.
- 15-second montage: anvil socket → quartz → diamond → emerald → title.
- Thumbnail candidate: anvil, pickaxe, and five gemstones; use the original icon as reference, not as an edit target.
