Artisan's Sockets adds focused Vanilla+ equipment customization without introducing a new crafting station or client-side dependency. Drop supported equipment and one gemstone on an anvil to apply a permanent specialization.

## How socketing works

1. Place or drop a supported tool, weapon, or armor piece on top of an anvil.
2. Drop quartz, an amethyst shard, a diamond, an emerald, or lapis lazuli beside it.
3. The gemstone is consumed.
4. The equipment receives the matching socket name, lore, components, and vanilla attribute bonuses.

Each supported item receives one specialization. The mechanic is intended for vanilla equipment; compatibility with custom equipment from other datapacks depends on how that equipment stores its components.

## Gemstone paths

| Gemstone | Tools and weapons | Armor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quartz | Automatically smelts supported ore drops | Adds toughness and knockback resistance |
| Amethyst | Adds movement speed while held | Adds movement speed while worn |
| Diamond | Adds attack damage and mining efficiency | Adds maximum health and toughness |
| Emerald | Adds block and entity interaction reach | Adds safe-fall distance and step height |
| Lapis lazuli | Adds mining and underwater efficiency | Adds oxygen and underwater mining bonuses |

## Why use it

The sockets create distinct equipment roles without replacing the vanilla enchantment table, smithing table, or anvil. A mining tool can become an auto-smelting workhorse, a travel set can emphasize movement, and combat gear can trade toward damage or durability-related attributes.

## Installation

1. Download one language version.
2. Place the ZIP directly in `<world>/datapacks/`.
3. Enter the world or run `/reload`.
4. Test socketing with one supported item, one gemstone, and an anvil.

Do not install the English and Russian ZIPs together; they are alternative localizations.
